About Triund

Triund is a popular trekking destination located in the Kangra district of Himachal Pradesh, India. Situated at the foothills of the Dhauladhar ranges, this scenic spot offers breathtaking views of the Kangra Valley and snow-capped mountains.

Trek Details

  • Location: Triund, McLeod Ganj, Dharamshala, Himachal Pradesh
  • Base Camp: Gallu Devi Temple (starting point)
  • Altitude: 2,827 meters (9,275 ft)
  • Trek Distance: 9 km round trip (5.6 miles)
  • Duration: 4-5 hours ascent, 2-3 hours descent
  • Difficulty: Easy to moderate
  • Trail Type: Well-marked rocky path with some steep sections

Key Attractions

  • 360° Mountain Views: Panoramic vistas of Dhauladhar range and Kangra Valley
  • Sunset Point: Famous for spectacular Himalayan sunsets
  • Stargazing: Crystal-clear night skies perfect for astronomy
  • Snow Line: Winter snow views (Dec-Feb)
  • Forest Trail: Oak and rhododendron woods with wildlife sightings
  • Magic View Cafe: Popular stop midway with refreshments

Camping Details

  • Campsite Location: Designated flat area at Triund top
  • Facilities: Basic tents with sleeping bags (available for rent)
  • Best Season: March-June & September-November
  • Winter Note: Camping possible but extremely cold (below 0°C)
  • Food: Local dhabas serve simple meals (dal-rice, maggi, tea)
  • Toilets: Basic pit toilets available

Essential Tips for Trekkers

  • Best Time: March-June (spring/summer) and September-November (autumn)
  • Monsoon: Avoid July-August (leech infestation, slippery trails)
  • Winter: December-February (snow trek, requires proper gear)
  • Packing List:
    • Sturdy trekking shoes
    • Layered clothing (temperature drops at night)
    • Rain jacket (weather changes quickly)
    • Headlamp/flashlight
    • Basic first aid kit
    • Water bottles (2L minimum)
    • Energy snacks/dry fruits
  • Permits: Not required for Indian nationals
  • Guides: Available at McLeod Ganj (₹1000-1500 per group)
  • Mobile Network: BSNL/Vodafone works intermittently

How to Reach Triund

  • Nearest Airport: Gaggal Airport, Dharamshala (20 km from McLeod Ganj)
  • Nearest Railway: Pathankot Railway Station (90 km away)
  • By Road:
    • From Delhi: 10-12 hrs drive to McLeod Ganj
    • From Chandigarh: 6-7 hrs drive
  • Starting Point: Gallu Devi Temple (3 km from McLeod Ganj square)
  • Taxi Options: Available from McLeod Ganj to Gallu Devi (₹300-400)

Nearby Places to Explore

  • Bhagsu Nag Temple: Sacred waterfall (2 km from McLeod Ganj)
  • Dalai Lama Temple: Tibetan spiritual center
  • St. John in the Wilderness: Historic church
  • Naddi Viewpoint: Sunset watching spot
  • Dharamkot: Hippie village with cafes

Safety Information

  • Altitude Sickness: Rare but possible - stay hydrated
  • Weather: Can change rapidly - carry warm layers
  • Trail Markings: Follow red/white paint marks on rocks
  • Emergency Contacts:
    • McLeod Ganj Police: +91-1892-221338
    • Nearest Hospital: Civil Hospital Dharamshala
  • Leave No Trace: Carry back all waste
